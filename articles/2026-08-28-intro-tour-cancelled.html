Festival and Party Replace Intro Tour this Semester

News Desk · 28 August 2026

The annual Intro Tour organised by Intro Team has been cancelled this year due to a lack of funding. The multi-day trip, which has traditionally welcomed new students to university life, will not take place in 2026.

In its place, Intro Team is hosting an Intro Party again, which was cancelled last year to make room for the Intro Festival. Because the tour has been cancelled, there are now enough resources for both events, with the Intro Festival set to be the biggest study-start event of the year. It will include a full day of activities and events run by student organisations, ending with music in the evening.

This year’s Intro Festival will be held at Ringriderpladsen, a more central location than last year’s event, making it easier for students to reach. Intro Team is hoping for a high turnout.
