Two New Business Masters Starting in September

News Desk · 28 August 2026

SDU Sønderborg is adding two new master’s programmes at the business faculty this autumn. The MSc in Global Supply Chain Management and the MSc in Marketing, Social Media and Digitalization are both running for the first time this year.

The supply chain programme, headed by Simon Sølvsten, has enrolled three students in its first cohort. It runs as a one-year master’s — a format designed to get students into their careers faster — and focuses on building resilient supply chains in the face of climate shocks, cyber threats, and geopolitical disruption.

The marketing and digitalization programme, led by Oliver Schnittka, starts with ten students. It carries AACSB accreditation and covers digital marketing, social media strategy, and the use of AI as a competitive tool. The programme emphasises small class sizes, data analysis skills, and close collaboration with industry partners.

The additions are also good news for students on the BSc in Economics and Business Administration at SDU Sønderborg — an internationally oriented, English-taught programme that draws students from across Europe. Previously, graduates looking to continue into a business master’s had to relocate. With these two programmes now available locally, they have a pathway to master’s-level study without leaving Sønderborg.
