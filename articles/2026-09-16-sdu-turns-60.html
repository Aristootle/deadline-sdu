SDU turns 60, commemoration held on campus

By Máté Sellei · 16 September 2026

The University of Southern Denmark opened for students sixty years ago on August 31st, 1966. To commemorate the anniversary, the rector Jens Ringsmose and other important personnel of the university are touring different campuses and Sønderborg is no exception. The event was held on the 16th of September from around 10 in the morning.

With a sizeable crowd, the speech from the rector began. He started by reflecting on the humble beginning of the university, back when it was the Odense Technical College. Then he went on to describe how due to a changing world, the university also went through dramatic change. He noted how the city of Sønderborg would not have been the same, if not for the university and the students that entered it.

“Here we are with a university that has become a part of the city.”

— Jens Ringsmose, Rector

Lastly, he thanked the researchers and students of SDU for shaping the university to what it is today.

Hanna Brüggemann, a mechanical engineering student, spoke about how "SDU is truly an exceptional place to study." She said that the uniqueness of SDU Sønderborg comes from the variety of the students, thanks to their international backgrounds. This fact also contributes to the variety of student organisations present throughout campus.

The campus dean, Henrik Bindslev then talked about the history of the university, how the campus here in Sønderborg came to be and how the business faculty got started, which is "a hallmark of the university". The Institute for Border Regions' integration into SDU gave way to what became the European Studies programme.

The dean also spoke about the international nature of SDU Sønderborg and how it makes the campus here so special. The variety of people all from different backgrounds here can be an inspiration for everywhere around the world.
