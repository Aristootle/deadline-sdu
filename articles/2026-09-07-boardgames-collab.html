Four Student Organisations Create a Day of Games, Crafting and Dance

Students gather for board games in the STEAR area of Alsion.

Ariana Kalsbeek · 07 September 2026

Last Sunday, the STEAR area of Alsion was filled with board games, knitting, tea and dancing as four student organisations came together for an afternoon of activities. SDU Boardgames, Tengoku, D&D League and The Yarn Society hosted the collaborative event, which brought both new and returning students into the same space.

Participants and organisers moved between board games, Warhammer, crocheting and Just Dance, making it possible to try something new or settle into a familiar hobby. The result was an afternoon that mixed playing, crafting and dancing without separating the activities into distinct groups.

“We had four organisations just vibing, and both participants and staff going from board games to Warhammer to knitting to dancing. It felt very harmonious.”

— Piotr, SDU Boardgames

For the organisers, that shared atmosphere was the event’s main success. By working together, the four clubs created a welcoming space where students could move freely between different interests and spend the afternoon finding their own way into the community.

SDU Boardgames plans to continue hosting board game events every other Sunday. The events are open to all students, and everyone is welcome to join, even without a group to play with; there is always a spot available at one of the tables.
