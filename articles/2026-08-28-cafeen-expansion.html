Caféen is Being Expanded, Events Temporarily Move Outside

Photo: Máté Sellei

By Ariana Kalsbeek · 28 August 2026

Caféen, SDU Sønderborg’s student bar, has been under construction for an expansion since the beginning of summer, and the work is still underway when the semester begins. The first Caféen event is still planned for August 28, but will not be held in the bar itself. Instead, organisers say they are likely to open outside or in temporary locations while building work continues.

The shift is part of a wider plan to keep student events running while the usual venue is being finished. The team says it has been in talks with dorm presidents and the university about how to maintain the student experience during the first weeks of term. For September, the plan is to stay open every Friday and host events in different locations across Sønderborg until the expanded Caféen is ready.

“We believe Caféen is so much more than it’s location, and are preparing to bring that anywhere we go.”

— Caféen team

University support has been central to the project. The institution is financing the expansion and working with Caféen on planning, architecture, and timing. The delay is a consequence of the building work, but the team said they are grateful for the university’s collaboration and the value it sees in Caféen as part of student life at SDU Sønderborg.

Once complete, the expanded bar is expected to offer more space for student events, a dedicated dance floor, a lounge area, and a games area. The new venue will also have more toilets and a larger bar, which should reduce queues and shorten wait times for students during busy nights.

While Caféen is closed, the main concern is not just the loss of a physical space, but the loss of familiarity for new students arriving in the city. The message to students disappointed by the timing is that the delay is necessary in order to get the final venue right.

Recruitment is continuing despite the temporary disruption. Caféen says it is still looking for new members who can adapt to changing locations, work well in a team, and help create a strong start to the semester. The best-case scenario is that the timeline stays on track and the new bar opens in time for regular Friday nights; the worst-case is another delay which could have consequences for larger events like the Christmas dinner.
