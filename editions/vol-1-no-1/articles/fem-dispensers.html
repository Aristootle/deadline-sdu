FEM Installs Menstrual Product Dispensers Across Campus Bathrooms

News Desk · Vol. 1, No. 1

The symbol FEM places on bathroom doors to mark facilities with a free dispenser. Image: FEM

After a period of inactivity, the student organisation FEM has relaunched with a new board — and one of its first moves has been a practical one. Three free menstrual product dispensers have been installed in bathrooms across campus, each stocked with both tampons and pads.

A door symbol marks which bathrooms carry a dispenser. FEM frames the initiative around basic access: “menstrual products are a human right,” the organisation says.
