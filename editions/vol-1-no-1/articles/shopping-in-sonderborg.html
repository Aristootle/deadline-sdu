Shopping in Sønderborg

By Sellei Máté · Vol. 1, No. 1

Moving to a new place is always difficult. Moving to a new country altogether is even more difficult to say the least. Being thrown into a brand new environment with almost no time to prepare before the first semester begins is no fun. Where do I buy furniture? Where do I get new clothing?

Worry not, as this article will guide you through the basics of shopping in Sønderborg and try to answer the questions that may arise.

Convenience stores

These are the stores that you will most often go to for your daily needs. In the city centre you can find Føtex and Netto* close to each other. The Føtex has bigger variety, also having a bakery and kiosk right outside the store as well. However, Netto is often cheaper and is very importantly open until 22:00, which is pretty much as late as you can shop in Sønderborg besides a few exceptions, which will be talked about later in the article.

Around the outer shopping area lie a variety of stores. One of these is Lidl, with a rotating international week, such as French, American and “Eastern European”, from where you may easily satisfy the home-craving you may get. The biggest store – Bilka – is found nearby on a hill. There is a great deal of variety to be found here, with a separate grocery section and a more miscellaneous one. Close to the university, a Rema 1000** is located, where you can stock up in-between classes during a long day.

Second-hand stores

Around the city, many second-hand stores can be found. These are places where one may go to purchase items left there by others. Most of these things are very convenient for new students, who are just moving in, as there is a wide range of furniture, foodware, stylish clothing and other gadgets and trinkets that could be used later.

But what if, for example, you buy a bed from one of these places, but don’t have the means to carry it back to your new place? No need to worry, as most of the second-hand stores have services that deliver the items to your desired destination, for a relatively small sum in turn.

Just to give some examples of second-hand stores, there is the Røde Kors Butik – naturally operated by the Red Cross – and the Nørrekobbel Genbrugsmarked, which is a large store where you can buy salvaged items from previous owners. Next up is Kirppu, which is a self-described “flea-supermarket”. Here, sellers rent out their unwanted belongings and sell them to interested customers, the store itself basically acting like a mediator.

Miscellaneous stores

Besides regular convenience stores and the second-hand stores scattered around Sønderborg, there are some that do not fit either of those names. One of these places is the Borgen shopping mall, which houses a number of different stores and municipal offices. Føtex was mentioned before and is located here, but fashion stores, food courts and even a cinema are located in Borgen.

Another one is Købmanden 24/7, which – as the name implies – is open all the time and can be accessed through an app called “Unlimit Key” (available both on Android and iOS). The store is very convenient for those living close by and there are quite a lot of dormitories around it. However, the prices are ever so slightly higher as a trade-off, so keep that in mind.

Naturally, there are plenty of gas stations, but it is important to mention Q8 as it is open the longest (23:00) in the city besides the previously mentioned 24/7. There are also plenty of small grocery stores scattered throughout the city operated by locals. These places are usually quite affordable, may offer some more exotic wares and often have fresh produce. An example would be Grøntland.

Online shopping

Lastly, it does not hurt to mention that there are a number of online options available to use. Vinted is one that many people might already be familiar with. It is mostly for clothing and accessories, so it will be rather useful when trying to restock the wardrobe. The other website that will be mentioned is dba.dk. It is a Danish website where anyone can sell and buy whatever was put up there by others. The range of what one can get is quite varied, so it is worth checking out, whether to get furniture or a bike for example.
