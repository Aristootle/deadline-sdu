Deadline Is Holding a Photo Competition

Vol. 1, No. 1

We are hosting a photo competition and we want you to take part in it!

Scan the QR code to vote in the photo competition.

The first theme of the photo competition is special as it is about Sønderborg. This goes out for both our newcomers and oldies, but also for Deasline itself as we start up and find our place here in the city and the community that surrounds us. The best submissions are going to be displayed in the next issue of Deadline.

In the spirit of that previously mentioned communality, the ranking would be decided by you, the readers! The voting process will be taking place from now until midnight of the 21st of September.

Submissions should be sent to Sellei Máté with the title of “Deadline Photo competition”. If you have any questions, feel free to write an email or reach out to us through our socials such as our Facebook and Instagram.

As said, the voting is LIVE and you can access it through this link or the QR code in the issue.

Have fun taking pictures!
