623 New Students at SDU Sønderborg

News Desk · Vol. 1, No. 1

This autumn, 623 students have been offered a place at SDU Sønderborg — 121 fewer than in 2025, representing a decline of around 16 percent. The campus received a total of 4,285 applications, of which 1,170 were first-priority.

Engineering remains the backbone of the campus. Of the 623 offered places, 438 are across the eight engineering programmes, which continue to attract the majority of incoming students.

SDU points to stricter admission requirements in both quota 1 and quota 2, as well as fewer available spots on certain programmes, as contributing factors. Nationally, SDU as a whole saw a smaller decline of three percent — 136 fewer places, for a total of 4,279 — meaning Sønderborg's drop is considerably steeper than the university average.

"There can be fluctuations from year to year, and on a smaller campus even relatively limited changes can show up clearly in the statistics," said Stinne Hørup Hansen, Deputy Director of Studies at SDU. She emphasised that Sønderborg remains a central campus for the university, with strong industry ties and an international study environment.

Earlier this summer, applications to SDU's bachelor's programmes rose eight percent nationally to 22,184, with five of six faculties seeing growth. European applicants showed particular enthusiasm: applications from the Nordic countries, EU and EEA were up nearly 40 percent compared with 2025. That broad international interest is worth noting for Sønderborg, which has long positioned itself as SDU's most internationally oriented campus. The final intake figures, however, tell a more mixed story for the campus specifically.
