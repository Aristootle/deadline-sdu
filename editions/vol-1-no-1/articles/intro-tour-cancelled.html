Intro Tour Cancelled — Party Replaces Annual Event

News Desk · Vol. 1, No. 1

The annual Intro Tour organised by Intro Team has been cancelled this year due to a lack of funding. The multi-day trip, which has traditionally welcomed new students to university life, will not take place in 2026.
