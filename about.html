Who We Are

The people behind Deadline and what we stand for.

Deadline is the independent student newspaper of the University of Southern Denmark, Sønderborg campus. We were founded in 2026 by a group of students who believed the campus needed a publication that reported on university life with honesty, curiosity, and a little ambition. We are editorially independent of the university administration. We publish once per month, with shorter dispatches on our social channels in between.

Editorial Policy

Deadline is committed to fair, accurate, and independent reporting. We do not represent the views of SDU or its administration. Opinion pieces are clearly labelled and represent the views of their authors or, in the case of editorials, of the Editorial Board collectively.

We correct errors promptly and transparently. If you believe we have made a factual error, please contact the editor-in-chief at arkal22@student.sdu.dk and we will review it as a priority.

We welcome contributions from all students. If you have a story tip, a letter to the editor, or would like to write for us, get in touch using the details below.
